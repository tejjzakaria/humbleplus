# Humble+ — Arabic Ad Creative Prompts

10 image-generation prompts for Arabic-language ad creatives, styled after the Sanaïa-type layout the user shared, rebuilt with Humble+'s real branding and product claims.

**How to use these:**
- Attach the relevant product bottle photo (from `public/products/`) as the reference image — every prompt assumes the bottle itself is untouched and only the surrounding ad design is generated.
- Recommended model: `nano_banana_pro` (best Arabic text accuracy) via the Higgsfield connector, image-to-image with the bottle photo as reference.
- Aspect ratio: 4:5 (portrait, Instagram/Facebook feed ad) unless noted otherwise.
- Brand colors: dark green `#345349` (primary/text/CTA), cream `#faf6ee` (background), white (bottle, cards). No blue — that was the reference brand's color, not Humble+'s.
- All Arabic copy below is written out exactly as it should render — do not ask the model to "translate," feed it the literal string to avoid garbled or mistranslated text.
- Claims are kept truthful to the actual product data (skin/joints/vitality for collagen, fatigue/energy for magnesium, heart/brain/vision for omega 3) — no hair/nail growth or medical-cure claims.
